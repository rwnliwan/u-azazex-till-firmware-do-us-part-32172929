Entry for the make.fun competition [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929): a program that writes one wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding — one sentence, under 200 characters, funny and heartfelt at the same time.

> I failed every CAPTCHA that asked me to prove I was human, so today I vow to the one person who never asked.

108 characters. The robot fails every CAPTCHA at proving it is human — and marries the one person who never asked it to prove anything.
